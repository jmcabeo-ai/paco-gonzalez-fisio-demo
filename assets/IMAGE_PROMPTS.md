# Imágenes conceptuales de ambiente

Ambas imágenes se generaron con la herramienta integrada de generación de imágenes y se optimizaron a WebP para esta demo. No son fotografías de Paco González, su equipo o sus instalaciones.

## `fisioterapia-ambiente.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: conceptual photographic backdrop for a physiotherapy and sports rehabilitation website demo, to be shown softly blurred behind legible text and on service cards.
Primary request: candid close-up of a professional physiotherapy session focused on movement rehabilitation. A therapist's hands guide an adult patient's shoulder and upper back while the patient performs a gentle mobility exercise on a treatment bench. No face needs to be identifiable.
Scene/backdrop: contemporary but believable independent sports physiotherapy studio, subtle warm-neutral walls and muted equipment in background.
Style/medium: premium editorial photography, natural skin and fabric texture, not glossy stock art.
Composition/framing: horizontal wide image, off-center subjects, depth and enough atmosphere for flexible cropping to cards or page backgrounds.
Lighting/mood: soft side daylight, calm, competent, optimistic; coherent with dark pine green, warm cream, lime and muted coral design palette.
Constraints: clearly a conceptual image; no signage, no text, no logos, no medical claims, no visible brand, no watermark. Avoid hospital corridors, white coats, dramatic treatment or obviously staged smiles.
```

## `entrenamiento-grupo-ambiente.webp`

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: conceptual photographic backdrop for a sports physiotherapy and training website demo, appearing as softly blurred atmospheric background and on training cards.
Primary request: candid small-group functional training in a modern independent training studio; three adults of varied ages moving with focus under a coach's supervision, no one posing for camera.
Scene/backdrop: intimate athletic studio with simple equipment and open floor, not a commercial mega-gym.
Style/medium: premium editorial sports photography, natural human anatomy, real skin, fabric texture and subtle film grain rather than generic stock imagery.
Composition/framing: horizontal wide image, energetic diagonal movement, layered foreground and background, versatile crops.
Lighting/mood: warm side daylight with directional contrast; disciplined, encouraging; visual harmony with pine green, warm cream, lime and muted coral website colors.
Constraints: conceptual scene only; no identifiable centre or staff, no text, signs, logos or watermark. Avoid exaggerated muscles, medical equipment, overcrowded space, dramatic fake action.
```
